KEV Analytics Dashboard — Power BI

Multi-page Power BI dashboard integrating CVSS, EPSS, KEV status, and remediation-age across enterprise vulnerability scan data — with a full executive briefing package. Built during my DHS HS-POWER internship on the CBP Vulnerability Assessment Team.

Power BI Analytics Federal
Publication policy. Screenshots and datasets from the CBP environment are non-public. This page describes the objectives, dashboard design, analytics process, and technologies — with no real scan output, host counts, CVE lists, or system names.

Objectives

  • Give operators and executives one place to see enterprise vulnerability posture through a risk-based lens, not a severity-only lens.
  • Surface the gap between CVSS severity and real-world exploitation likelihood (EPSS) across the KEV-matched vs. non-KEV population.
  • Track remediation velocity against CISA BOD 26-04 risk-based timelines and highlight outlier systems and aged-finding backlogs.
  • Deliver a briefing that leadership can act on — clear takeaways, not a dashboard tour.

Dashboard design

Four connected pages, each answering a specific question a leader or operator asks in the first 30 seconds:

  1. Executive overview — total open findings, KEV-matched count and share, average remediation days, BOD 26-04 compliance snapshot.
  2. KEV vs. non-KEV comparison — side-by-side distributions of CVSS, EPSS, and remediation-age; the "severity is flat, exploitation isn't" chart.
  3. Concentration & ownership — where KEVs cluster by system group, vendor, and product family; who owns remediation.
  4. Aged-finding backlog — findings past their BOD 26-04 due date, sorted by risk score for immediate action.

Analytics process

  • Data ingest: Nessus/Tenable scan output joined against the public CISA KEV Catalog by CVE ID. EPSS pulled from FIRST.
  • Data model: star schema — a fact table of scan findings, dimensions for Asset, CVE, KEV metadata, and Time.
  • Measures (DAX): KEV match rate, weighted risk score, remediation-age buckets, BOD 26-04 compliance %.
  • Weighted risk score: KEV status (0.40) + Asset Criticality (0.25) + EPSS (0.20) + CVSS (0.20) — see the KEV / BOD 26-04 case study for the full model.
  • Refresh: scheduled refresh against the scan pipeline; incremental refresh for large tables.

Key insights (methodology-level, sanitized)

  • Severity is flat; exploitation isn't. Mean CVSS is nearly identical across KEV and non-KEV populations, but EPSS is dramatically higher in the KEV group — the case for KEV-first ordering, in one chart.
  • KEV concentration is not uniform. Findings cluster on specific vendors and product families — remediation focus produces disproportionate risk reduction.
  • Aged findings drive the majority of business risk. A small share of overdue KEV findings represents the majority of BOD 26-04 exposure at any given moment.
